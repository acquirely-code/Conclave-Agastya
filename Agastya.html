The reframe

The question isn't whether AI will change your school.

It's what is left when it does.

In India, over 50% of leading higher-education institutions already use generative AI to build learning materials, 40% run AI tutoring systems, and 38% use it for automated grading.

Source: EY-Parthenon & FICCI, "Future-Ready Campuses," 2025

The schools that thrive in the next decade will be built around what AI can't teach. This Conclave is where you learn to build them, by experiencing it yourself.

About Agastya

Every child is naturally curious. Education should build on that.

That belief started Agastya in 1999, when Ramji Raghavan brought together a group of scientists, educators and entrepreneurs around a single idea: children learn through hands-on exploration, and rote memorisation quietly takes that away from them.

Today we're one of India's leading education non-profits, and the world's largest creativity laboratory, reaching over 40 million children and 300,000+ teachers across 24 states through mobile science labs, innovation hubs, campus creativity labs, science centres and digital learning platforms.

FAQ

Questions leaders ask.

How do I get to Kuppam?

Fly into Bengaluru or Chennai. Campus transfers are arranged from the nearest arrival points, and full travel instructions go to every registered leader.

What's included in the ticket?

Everything except your travel to Kuppam: two nights' campus accommodation, all meals from welcome lunch to farewell lunch, full access to the labs and the 172-acre campus, every session, the AI Experience Lab, all Action Labs, your Readiness Diagnostic and 90-day plan, certificate, and post-conclave community access.

Is this for teachers?

No. This is a leadership room: school founders and owners, principals and heads of school, academic and curriculum heads. People who can decide what changes on Monday.

Do I need to know anything about AI already?

No. The AI Experience Lab starts where you are. Leaders who have never opened an AI tool and leaders already piloting them both leave with the same thing: a clear map of where it helps their school and where it fails.

When is the full agenda out?

Soon. Registered leaders receive it first, along with speaker announcements as they're confirmed.

What if I need to cancel or send someone else?

Tickets are non-cancellable. If you're unable to attend, you may transfer your ticket to someone else by sharing your registration details with them.

Who am I paying, and what appears on my statement?

The Conclave is hosted by Agastya International Foundation and delivered with its associate organisation, Navam Foundation, which securely processes event registrations. Your payment goes to Navam Foundation and your card statement will reflect that. Your seat is confirmed by Agastya.
